Mobile App Development

Mobile app development is the process of creating applications that run on mobile devices (Android, iOS, or both). Apps can be:

  • Native (built specifically for Android or iOS)
  • Cross-platform (a single codebase works on both Android & iOS)
  • Web-based (progressive web apps - PWAs)
