Introducing Our Business Models

Flexible engagement strategies designed to fit your business needs, timelines, and budgets.

Extended Team

This model is cost-effective as it does not require any investment in infrastructure and here the client gets the chance to hire the whole development team and remote them accordingly. The hiring of the team is completely contract-based and after all the constancy is due after the completion of the project.

Time and Material

In the Time and Material model, the client is supposed to pay for the time taken for completion of the project additionally with all materials used. This is also a cost-effective process again as no requirement of investment. Moreover, Time and material model follows Agile methodologies.

Fix Scope Model

In this model, the complete project will be done by us as per the requirement of our client in the fixed budget. Through the Fix scope model, the client will receive the best development solutions, all under the fixed budget.
